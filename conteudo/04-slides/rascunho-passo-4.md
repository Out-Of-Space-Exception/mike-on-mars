## Slide 2 · Problem

**Title:** Real Choices, Not Flashcards

- Space is harsh: kids know Mars is cold and dusty, but rarely experience what it takes to survive there.
- Gamified education often feels like homework disguised as trivia instead of engaging play.
- True mission decisions require balance: every gain has a cost (solar energy vs. dust storms, shielding vs. power).

**Visuals & Media:**
- Suggested image: Real photo of Curiosity measuring gale dust or extreme Mars environment (`Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS`).
- Highlight callout: *"How do we make 7 to 12-year-olds understand real space trade-offs without overwhelming them?"*

## Slide 3 · Solution

**Title:** Grow, Collect, and Survive

- **Zero barriers:** Runs directly in any mobile web browser with no login, no download, and no child data collected.
- **Engaging loop:** Inspired by modern "grow & collect" favorites (like *Grow a Garden*), keeping children hooked.
- **Persistent world:** The Martian base stays saved on the device and crops continue growing even when closed.

**Visuals & Media:**
- Suggested image: Screenshot of the base gameplay showing the greenhouse, solar panels, and the Ingenuity companion.

---

## Slide 4 · How It Works

**Title:** Simple Systems, Deep Science

- **Only 3 Core Bars:** Energy ⚡, Oxygen 🫁, and Shield 🛡️ (solar radiation defense). No instant game over — low bars only slow down actions.
- **Build & Plant:** Place solar panels or RTG batteries; grow real space crops (lettuce, radishes, hatch chiles).
- **Explore & React:** Pauses for dynamic events (solar storms, dust storms, freezing nights) where every choice has a upside and downside.

**Visuals & Media:**
- Suggested image: Screenshot of the gameplay HUD with the 3 resource bars and an incoming Donki solar storm event prompt.

## Slide 5 · NASA Data

**Title:** Powered by Authentic NASA Science

- **Real Space Weather:** Live DONKI API space weather database triggers authentic solar storms measured on Mars.
- **40 Real Mission Cards:** Discoveries sealed in Perseverance-style sample tubes, from Gale Crater olivine to ancient mud cracks.
- **Verified Facts as Rewards:** Every card delivers an authentic 1-line fact and verified source from `nasa.gov` and `jpl.nasa.gov`.

**Visuals & Media:**
- Showcase of 3 actual NASA card photos:
  - Zinnia in space (`Photo: NASA / Scott Kelly`)
  - Curiosity sulfur rocks (`Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS`)
  - Mars blue sunset in Gale Crater (`Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS/Texas A&M`)

## Production notes

- This is a script, not proof that the planned captures have been recorded. Use only functioning features in the published game. Existing still images can be used for a reference edit, but must not be animated to invent interface behavior.
- For the final English video, capture the game in English. Existing Portuguese screenshots may serve as temporary reference material only.
- Keep the real event date visible and identify DONKI as archived data. Resource costs and shielding are simplified game rules, not NASA measurements.
- Give the three-action sequence clear cuts; opening a card follows exploration and is not a guaranteed reward for surviving a storm. If five seconds is too short, prioritize one legible card reveal over showing every animation.
- Use an adult human narrator or explicitly credited synthetic voice. Do not include minors' faces or voices, NASA logos, or uncredited audio. Preserve the visible "Made with AI" notice if using AI-generated visual/video material.
- Narrated captions may exceed the earlier suggestion of approximately seven words per scene because they match the spoken text. Check reading speed and the exact exported duration. If the team prefers shorter captions, shorten the voice-over to match.
- Pattern informed by the verified PureFlow transcript and inspected Zumorroda-X frames, plus the project descriptions. HerCode's full video sequence was not accessible; this draft does not claim an identical sequence across all three winners.
